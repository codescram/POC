Monthly Report Tomorrow’s India 1stAug 2025 –31stAug 2025

CONTENT INSIDE

This document helps you to analyze monthly progress of Digital Marketing activities. It also Compares the analytics with old data.

REPORTS HIGHLIGHTS
  • Reviews were added on the GMB; Business interactions slightly increased whereas the rating was maintained.
  • 922 users viewed the Business listing with 66% traffic from mobile as compared to last month's numbers -679
    • users with 48% traffic from mobile.
  • Bounce rate decreased to 50.85% from 62.83%. Overall traffic was maintained at user number more than 1 Lac (132445 users) which is a slight increase from last month's numbers (128479 users)
  • Major website traffic came from Delhi, Mumbai and Lucknow.
  • Most of the traffic came from the Homepage in the month of August.
  • Audience Demographics: 72.2% male, 27.8% female, with majority aged between 18-14 age followed by 25-34 age
    • group.
  • Google webmaster clicks were maintained while impressions slightly increased.
  • Facebook page likes saw an increase whereas Instagram's follower number saw a slight increase.
  • 2 Display ads were run in the month of August: Brand-centric and B2B communication.
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Maintain Post Reach & Engagement – Run minimal ads on all posts to ensure consistent visibility and interaction.
  • Enhance Social Media Engagement & Reach – Increase post engagement and reach to strengthen the brand’s social media presence. The ads sheet needs to be planned accordingly.
  • Strengthen Organic Reach – Prioritize SEO efforts and explore alternative content strategies to compete with paid ads.
  • B2B Tone – To focus on the B2B communication on LinkedIn and on Display Ads.
  • B2B Landing Page – Vibes team shared a Business-centric mockup of a LP. To focus on getting the same into action.
  • Campaign – To build on the LinkedIn profile and page in the month of Sep and plan for the podcast campaign.
